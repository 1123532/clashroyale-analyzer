Pro tips

Small habits that consistently help players climb.

⚡

Elixir discipline

Avoid leaking. If you’re unsure, cycle a cheap card safely.

🧠

Count their cycle

Track their win condition + key spell. Predict the next push.

🛡️

Defense → counterpush

Defend with minimal elixir, then convert surviving units into pressure.

🎬

Review losses

Watch 1 replay: find the first mistake (overcommit, bad spell, wrong cycle).
