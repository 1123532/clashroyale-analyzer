Pro Tips from Top Players

Master these fundamentals to climb faster

Elixir Management

Never leak elixir! Always have a plan to spend it efficiently and avoid going below 3 elixir unless defending.

Card Counting

Keep track of your opponent's cycle. Knowing when their win condition is coming helps you prepare counters.

Patience Wins Games

Don't overcommit in single elixir. Build advantages slowly and strike when you have elixir lead.

Defense into Offense

A good defense often leads to the strongest counter-pushes. Defend smart, then attack with survivors.

Know Your Matchups

Understand which decks counter yours and adjust your playstyle. Sometimes defense is the best offense.

Watch Your Replays

After a loss, watch the replay to understand what went wrong. Learn from every mistake.
